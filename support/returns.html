30-Day Exchange for Initial Failures
Any current model product that fails in the first 30 days after purchase from Strucktured will be exchanged for the same model.

  • Please call for a return authorization.
  • Product must be received back in original packaging with all accessories. Strucktured will exchange the "chassis only" in cases where original box and packing is unavailable. Restocking fees will be applied only if your product is not in the original box.
  • Unit must be in "like new" condition.
  • Opened satellite boxes & systems or any activated cards may not be returned. Exchanges can be done through the manufacturer.
  • Free shipping promos do not apply to products over 50 lbs.
  • Customers are responsible for return shipping unless the product arrives defective or damaged.
