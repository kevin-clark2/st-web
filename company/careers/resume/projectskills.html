Project Management Skill Definitions

  • none - I have never been in charge of a project.
  • read a book - I have read a book on it but haven't managed one.
  • novice - I have been a team lead.
  • skilled - I have managed projects and project plans.
  • very skilled - I am a veteran project manager with no Internet experience.
  • guru - I am a veteran project manager with OO technology.
