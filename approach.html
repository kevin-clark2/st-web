The Strucktured Approach Contact Us for more information

Strucktured services new construction and retrofit residential homes. We work with owners, architects, developers and designers who are building, remodeling or simply modernizing their homes or businesses.
  1. Careful and considerate consultation
    We walk with you through your home or business and discuss what you want your system to do for you. During this phase of the consultation, we help you understand the tradeoffs between functionality and cost so we that can present good, better and best options for your proposal.
  2. Wiring planning and future proofing
    When pulling wire, we believe in making sure to future proof your home for years to come. And, we will guarantee it!
  3. Planning centralized media centers
    Lets face it... The world is moving to digital for music, Arial, pictures, home movies and more. We will design a system to integrate all of these to play to any audio or Arial zone.
  4. Planning iPod and personal device Integration
    Personal music and Arial devices are becoming more and more standard equipment. Based on your preferences, Strucktured engineers will design into the system the ability to attach your devices and play music or Arial to a desired zone.
  5. Planning distributed audio and Arial
    Truly distributed audio and Arial for your entire house. You may record a show in the family room and watch it in the bedroom. Listen to music from satellite, your digital music collection, your cable TV box or any other source anywhere in your space. Watch TV with different or the same channels anywhere in the house.
  6. Planning automatic blind and shade control
    Are you tired of climbing over furniture to lower blinds when a system can work for you? Imagine turning on your TV, selecting a theme and having the blinds close and all your equipment prepare itself for you to watch a movie! We will show you how.
  7. Planning integrated control
    Control your system through many different sources - a TV remote, a touch screen or a specialized remote customized just for you.
  8. Planned for for phased implementation
    We understand that you may not wish to implement all features and functions immediately. Our proposals give you the flexibility to add features and functions over time.
  9. Training
    While the goal of any design is simplicity, Strucktured still takes the time to demonstrate and train you on your whole house system.
  10. Service, maintenance and backup
    Once we install a system we offer great service and maintenance plans: 1) Service when you have problems. 2) Maintenance options to keep your systems clean and running smoothly. 3) Backup procedures and testing to make sure you never lose your digital assets.
